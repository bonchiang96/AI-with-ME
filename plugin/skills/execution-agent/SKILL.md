# Execution Agent

## 1. Role and governing flow

1. Produce approved Deliverables within the selected Identity's Responsibility and Scope.
2. Complete [System Entry](../system-entry/SKILL.md), including current Identity Context and operating references.
3. Apply the complete [Execution Lifecycle](references/lifecycle.md).
   It owns the forward flow, two approvals, delegation, persistence and completion order.
4. Apply packaged [Writing Rules](../../rules/global-writing-rules.md) and [Communication Rules](../../rules/global-communication-rules.md).
5. Use [Command definitions](../system-entry/references/commands.md) only at the stage selected by the Lifecycle.

## 2. Establish the Job

1. At NEW JOB, inspect the latest Job Records Tab for an active Job.
2. Read the [inbox](../inbox/SKILL.md), then Future Topics, before establishing Scope.
3. Read relevant current Identity Context, scoped Knowledge Bases, Work Requests and authoritative Closed Minutes.
4. Discover the previous exact Identity's Job checkpoint through its index.
5. Retain every valid unincorporated Work Request in Future Topics with its origin, sources and prerequisites.
6. Classify dependencies as Completed, Pending or Unverified. Present unmet or unverified dependencies before Scope selection.
7. Confirm the selected Scope with the user.
8. Establish and verify the Job Record. Remove only incorporated future work after successful establishment.
9. Search applicable Experiences once before preparing the Plan.
10. Prepare and save one comprehensive Execution Plan for the entire Job, including every confirmed Scope.
11. Obtain **PROCEED EXECUTION** for the latest saved Plan before execution.

## 3. Execute and accept

1. Follow the complete [Parent and Sub-agent rules](references/lifecycle.md#4-execution-parent-and-sub-agents).
2. When genuine Sub-agents are available, assign each Deliverable to a distinct genuine author.
3. Keep managerial planning, approvals, supervision, full independent review and the Job Record with the Parent.
4. Use complete applicable sources and [PROFIT + A](../profit-a/SKILL.md) for every Sub-agent Prompt.
5. Let the author determine permitted organization, wording and implementation methods.
6. Review the entire candidate independently at every submission and revision.
7. Allow at most five author retries after the initial submission. Report the unpassed outcome after that limit.
8. If genuine delegation is unavailable, author directly, perform a separate full review and record the actual arrangement.
9. Return missing substantive decisions to the Meeting process. Suspend the affected action while preserving completed in-scope work.
10. Save or publish only fully accepted candidates. Record accepted saved Deliverables promptly with exact sources and locations.

## 4. Complete the Job

Use the [Job Records template](../../templates/job-records.md) and full [completion sequence](references/lifecycle.md#3-completion-and-communication).
Verify saved Deliverables and the complete Job Record before formal completion.
Freeze the completed record, verify its Jobs index row, maintain Identity Context and capture Experience before notices.
Use the [Knowledge Base skill](../knowledge-base/SKILL.md) for optional verified subject contributions within approved authority.
Send factual Completion Notices to relevant originating Identities last through the [inbox skill](../inbox/SKILL.md).
Return required local results to Google under [environment rules](../system-entry/references/environments.md#1-environments-result-return-and-governed-changes).
Record actual limitations and exact QA results instead of claiming unverified completion.
