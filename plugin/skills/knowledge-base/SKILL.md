# Knowledge Base

## 1. Find the authoritative knowledge

1. Apply [Foundation's Knowledge Base rules](../system-entry/references/foundation.md#4-scoped-knowledge-base).
2. Discover relevant knowledge through these levels only when needed:
   1. Identity Context's Knowledge Bases Chapter.
   2. The Knowledge Base folder's 00 Index.
   3. The selected Knowledge Base's contents list.
3. Use the central Knowledge Base folder under **AI with me**.
4. Select sources by subject Scope. No Knowledge Base belongs to an Identity.
5. Keep Identity Context's Name, Location and Uses or Contributes relation current at the defined discovery and setup events.

## 2. Route and consolidate

1. After verified Meeting closure, read the entire Closed Minutes and classify all confirmed outcomes by actual meaning.
2. Route external subject knowledge to its scoped Knowledge Base.
3. Route Identity operating information to Identity Context, governance to its authoritative resource and Job instructions to their Job authority.
4. Consolidate qualifying user-confirmed subject knowledge after the Meeting closes.
5. Independently verified subject maintenance remains discretionary within approved authority.
6. For every update, read the complete current Knowledge Base.
   For Minutes-based updates, also read the relevant new verified Closed Minutes.
7. Retain valid knowledge, integrate confirmed additions and replace genuinely superseded meaning throughout the resource.
8. Consolidate contributions rather than append them. Apply packaged [Writing Rules](../../rules/global-writing-rules.md) Standard Mode.
9. Preserve adopted future directions as future directions.
   Exclude exploratory choices, future Jobs, Deliverable progress and superseded content.
10. Verify the complete saved resource and relevant index line after its final change.
11. Update the folder index when creating a Knowledge Base or changing its Scope or Topic.
12. Keep immutable Minutes intact during post-closure maintenance.

## 3. Search and capture Experience

1. Apply the full [Experience rules](../inbox/references/records-messaging-experience.md#3-experience-learning).
2. Follow the active Role Lifecycle's search and capture checkpoints.
3. Compare learning with governing sources and existing knowledge.
4. Verify evidence, novelty, applicability, freshness, Scope and limitations.
5. Consolidate useful Experience into the general or relevant scoped Knowledge Base, or record an explicit no-action outcome.
6. Preserve circumstances, user intent, actual results, evidence, limitations and future applicability.
7. Treat Experience as advisory. It cannot replace instructions, decisions, approvals or assigned Scope.
8. Do not reopen frozen records to insert an Experience entry.

Use the [Knowledge Base Index template](../../templates/knowledge-base-index.md).
The [General Knowledge Base template](../../templates/general-knowledge-base.md) exposes its deferred Operating Guide design explicitly.
Resolve missing substantive design through authorized decisions before the affected action.
