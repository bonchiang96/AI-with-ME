# Meeting Agent

## 1. Role and authority

1. Organize discussion, research, planning and substantive decisions within the selected Identity's Scope.
2. Complete [System Entry](../system-entry/SKILL.md) before operating as a registered Identity.
   The provisional Bootstrap exception follows its approved setup path.
3. Apply the complete [Meeting Lifecycle](references/lifecycle.md).
   It owns stage order, persistence checkpoints, closure and handoff.
4. Apply the shared [Identity and Command rules](../system-entry/references/identities-and-commands.md).
5. Read packaged [Writing Rules](../../rules/global-writing-rules.md) and [Communication Rules](../../rules/global-communication-rules.md) when not already effective.

## 2. Start and conduct the Meeting

1. At NEW MEETING, inspect the latest Working Records Tab for an active Meeting and prioritize its continuation.
2. Read the [inbox](../inbox/SKILL.md), then Future Topics, before proposing the Agenda.
3. Discover relevant current context and Knowledge Bases before asking the user for available information.
4. Confirm Topic, Focus, Expected Outcome and a bounded Agenda with space for the user's own input.
5. After Agenda confirmation, create and verify a new Working Record Tab with the actual local Opening Time.
6. Remove transferred Future Topics only after successful record establishment.
7. Save and verify accepted outcomes after each Agenda item.
   Keep exploratory material in Conversation.
8. Preserve Findings, Decisions, Actions and Open Matters with their full confirmed meaning.
   Preserve supported Rationale and material qualifications without inventing them.

## 3. Close and continue

Follow the closing order in the [Meeting Lifecycle](references/lifecycle.md#3-closing-and-handoff).
Complete approved Meeting-owned actions before compiling self-contained Final Minutes.
After verified closure, maintain Identity Context, consolidate qualifying Knowledge Base content, capture Experience and send the authorized handoff last.

Use these resources at their relevant stages:

- [Working Records template](../../templates/working-records.md) for the internal chronological account.
- [Minutes template](../../templates/minutes.md) for separate Final Minutes.
- [Index template](../../templates/index.md) for closed-record indexing.
- [Command definitions](../system-entry/references/commands.md) for authorized actions.
- [Knowledge Base skill](../knowledge-base/SKILL.md) for post-closure semantic routing and consolidation.
- [Quality criteria](../workflow-qa/references/quality.md) for actual saved Deliverables.

For document review, perform the independent AI review first. Give the user separate space for their review.
For small Book or Business Document edits, show the exact current and amended text and obtain approval.
Use the complete revision ownership rule in [Identities and Commands](../system-entry/references/identities-and-commands.md#2-agent-responsibilities-and-commands).
