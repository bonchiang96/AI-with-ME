# Quality and Independent QA

## 1. Verify actual Deliverables

1. Apply the complete [Quality criteria](references/quality.md).
2. Review the latest saved complete artifact, not only its working copy or earlier approved draft.
3. Verify Content Quality, Writing Compliance and Formatting Compliance separately.
4. Apply packaged [Writing Rules](../../rules/global-writing-rules.md), the correct Writing Mode and the artifact's Operating Guide.
5. Record actual PASS or FAIL, permitted corrections and final saved-version verification.
6. Recheck the complete final artifact after substantive changes.
7. Apply the Working Record exception accurately: verify persistence without imposing mandatory document QA or a Quality Checklist.

## 2. Run independent QA as a Job

1. Establish the authorized QA Scope under the [Execution Agent Lifecycle](../execution-agent/references/lifecycle.md).
2. Inspect every approved item at the authorized depth with independent, complete and objective assessment.
3. Use a genuine Sub-agent where the approved arrangement and available capability permit.
4. Compare full source meaning, intent, constraints, qualifications and approved revisions with the actual target.
5. Identify omissions, weakening, excessive editing, unsupported additions and changed requirements.
6. Trace the earliest verified divergence and its downstream impact.
7. Report exact source and target references, criteria, evidence, findings and a useful correction starting point.
8. Use PASS, FAIL or Unable to Verify for each inspected item.
9. Preserve review-only boundaries. QA findings do not independently authorize edits.
10. Complete every approved item without cached PASS reuse or automatic skipping.

Independent QA may be review-only or periodic when authorized.
A completed audit may include failed or unverifiable findings.
Internal QA and Parent acceptance do not replace an independently requested QA Workflow.
