# 04 Minutes

## First Tab — Operating Guide

1. **Purpose.** Preserve self-contained Final Minutes separately from internal Working Records.
2. **Name.** Use **04 Minutes** in the Meeting identity folder.
3. **Metadata.** Each Meeting's Minutes includes the shared metadata and its Working Record Locator below.
4. **Structure.** Keep this guide in the first native Tab. Use one later Tab per Meeting's Final Minutes.
   Every Minutes Tab has the five Chapters below.
5. **Formatting.** Use readable native headings. Group the substantive Chapter by related subject rather than required Agenda chronology.
6. **Local QA.** Verify complete adopted meaning, sources, actions, dispositions and actual local times.
   Apply full Content, Writing and Formatting verification to the latest saved Minutes.
   Apply [Writing Rules](../rules/global-writing-rules.md), including the applicable Strict Mode requirements.

The [Meeting Lifecycle](../skills/meeting-agent/references/lifecycle.md) owns compilation, closure, indexing and handoff actions.
Verified closed Minutes are immutable.
The remaining scaffold represents a new Minutes Tab.
