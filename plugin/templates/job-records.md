# 03 Job Records

## Tab 1. Operating Guide

1. **Purpose.** Each Job Record is the Working Document of one Job.
   Once completed and frozen, it is the concise Proof of Job.
   The Execution Parent exclusively owns and writes it.

2. **Names.** The file name is 03 Job Records.
   It resides in an Execution Agent's identity folder.
   Each Job has one Tab. The Tab naming convention is pending design.

3. **Metadata.** Chapter 1 centralizes essential Job Metadata.
   It holds actual times for:

   - Scope Confirmation.
   - Plan Approval.
   - Execution Start.
   - Completion.

   It does not repeat Responsibility or the Record's own Locator.

4. **Sections.** Each Job Record Tab has five Chapters:

   1. Job Information.
   2. Confirmed Scope & Sources.
   3. Execution Plan & Approval.
   4. Execution & QA Log.
   5. Completion & Handoff.

5. **Formatting.** Use a Google Docs file.
   This Operating Guide is the first Tab.
   Each new Job is a new Tab after the latest Tab.
   The record is append-oriented.
   Chapter 4 is chronological.

6. **Local QA criteria.** Verify Content, Writing and Formatting separately. Check these points:

   1. Each confirmed Scope lists its exact Work Requests, Closed Minutes, sources and dependencies.
   2. The comprehensive Plan and its approval are preserved.
   3. Each accepted Deliverable keeps its execution and QA evidence.
   4. Work done without genuine Sub-agents is labeled as not delegated.
   5. Chapter 5 reports each Deliverable, its Scope and actual Activity.
   6. The five mandatory persistence checkpoints are saved and verified.
