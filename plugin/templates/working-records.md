# 03 Working Records

## First Tab — Operating Guide

1. **Purpose.** Preserve the Meeting Identity's internal chronological continuity and accepted Agenda outcomes.
2. **Name.** Use **03 Working Records** in the Meeting identity folder.
3. **Metadata.** Each Meeting Tab includes the shared metadata listed below.
4. **Structure.** Keep this guide in the first native Tab. Use one later Tab per authorized Meeting.
   Every Meeting Tab has the five Chapters below.
5. **Formatting.** Use readable native headings and an append-oriented Agenda account.
6. **Local QA.** Verify accurate persistence, accepted outcomes, exact sources and required checkpoints.
   A Working Record needs neither mandatory document Writing QA nor a Quality Checklist.

The [Meeting Lifecycle](../skills/meeting-agent/references/lifecycle.md) owns actions, persistence timing and closure.
The remaining scaffold represents a new Meeting Tab, not additional guide Chapters.
